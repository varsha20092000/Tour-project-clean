Our Policies

Welcome to TOUR365!

To ensure all our guests have a comfortable and enjoyable stay, please review our policies carefully.

1. Booking and Reservation Policy

  • Check-In Time: 3:00 PM
  • Check-Out Time: 11:00 AM
  • Minimum Stay: A two-night minimum stay is required on weekends and during peak seasons.
  • Payment: A 50% deposit is required to secure your booking, with the remaining balance due 7 days before arrival.
  • Age Requirement: Guests must be 21 years or older to book a reservation.

2. Cancellation Policy

  • Full Refund: Cancellations made at least 7 days before the arrival date will receive a full refund.
  • Partial Refund: Cancellations made within 7 days of the arrival date will receive a 50% refund.
  • No Refund: No refund will be issued for cancellations made within 24 hours of check-in.

3. Security Deposit

  • Deposit Requirement: A security deposit of $300 will be held and released within 3 days of check-out, provided there are no damages or policy violations.
  • Damage Fees: Any damage to the property or excessive cleaning required will be deducted from the security deposit.

4. House Rules

  • No Smoking: Smoking is strictly prohibited inside the chalet. A $200 cleaning fee will be charged for any violation.
  • No Pets: We love animals, but for the safety and comfort of all guests, pets are not allowed.
  • Quiet Hours: Quiet hours are from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM to respect our neighbors and other guests.
  • Maximum Occupancy: The maximum number of guests allowed is 8. Exceeding this limit may result in additional charges or cancellation of your stay.

5. Amenities and Usage

  • Wi-Fi: Complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the property.
  • Fire Pit: Guests are welcome to use the outdoor fire pit but must ensure it is fully extinguished before leaving the area.
  • Hot Tub (if available): Please adhere to the safety guidelines posted near the hot tub. Use is at your own risk.
  • Kitchen: The kitchen is fully equipped. We kindly ask guests to wash all dishes and return items to their original places before check-out.
